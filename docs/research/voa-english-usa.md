# Research — VOA «Так говорят в Америке» (English USA)

Facts observed on `golosameriki.com` (October 2026); the importer is `courses/voa-english-usa` (spec 03 §5).

## Site and licence

- No index page. Lessons are archive articles titled «Урок английского языка №N» (2001-08…2002-01), found
  through `/sitemap.xml` → `sitemap_434_1..8.xml.gz`: 104 lessons plus review lessons 52а and 104а.
  Lessons 34, 35, 86, 87 also exist as identical 2001-08 copies; lessons 1–31 were republished in 2009 as
  `learning-english-N` with a looser layout (not used). Lesson 85's article repeats lesson 86.
- Audio: `av.voanews.com/Videoroot/Archive/MediaAssets2/russian/<YYYY_MM>/Audio/mp3/russian_english_NNN.mp3`
  (redirects to voa-video.voanews.eu), mostly `2001_12`; 43–45, 95–97 in `2001_10`; 46–49, 98–101 in
  `2001_11`. Audio articles («Урок N», e.g. `/a/2034272.html`) exist only for some lessons.
- Licence (`/p/3981.html`, «Авторские права»): texts, audio and video by VOA staff are public domain;
  credit golosameriki.com / voanews.com or «Голос Америки». robots.txt allows `/a/*` and `/sitemap*`.

## Page structure

Article body with part labels: «Цель», «Содержание», Conversation (speaker names; sometimes
"Conversation #2"), Words and phrases (tables, or `<br>` lines with English and Russian split by a tab or
a space; headings noun/verb/adj; "Example: … / Например: …"), Grammar/Usage notes or Grammar Review,
Homework (translate, answer questions — no answers).
